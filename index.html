BEYOND WHAT'S SEEN

▪ ABOUT ME

I’m Bống, a graphic designer interested in the ideas behind what we see.

For me, design is not only about how something takes shape, but also about what gives that shape a reason to exist. I enjoy exploring the thoughts, details, and perspectives behind each project — then turning them into a visual language that feels intentional and distinct.

BEYOND THE SHAPE reflects this way of working. It is about looking past the finished form to understand the idea behind it, and finding the shape that belongs to that idea.

Every project starts from a different thought. The form is simply where that thought becomes visible.
