About Us
私たちについて

From
hand to hand.

We embrace the art of minimal design and a profound appreciation for organic pieces, aiming to revive the timeless beauty these objects have carried through the ages and reintroduce that beauty into our everyday lives.

For this packaging project, we accept organic shapes and also hold the principles to share its techniques and spirit. We primarily focus on sustainable materials, complemented by clean typography pieces for display and sale.

With deep respect for the traditions, craftsmanship, and aesthetic sensibilities cultivated by artisans over many generations, we devote ourselves to cherishing these legacies and passing them on for the future.
